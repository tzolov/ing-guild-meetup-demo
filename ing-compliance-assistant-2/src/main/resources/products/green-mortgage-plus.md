Product: Green Mortgage Plus (new product, planned launch Q1 2027, Netherlands and Germany)

A residential mortgage for homes with energy label A or B, with a 0.25% rate discount. The discount
is granted automatically when the AI onboarding assistant reads the energy certificate the customer
uploads. Pre-approval uses an in-house affordability model that scores the applicant on income,
spending patterns from 12 months of open-banking transaction data (with the customer's consent) and
the postcode of the property. Applicants scoring below the threshold are declined automatically and
receive a standard rejection e-mail. The onboarding assistant is a Gemini-based chat that answers
product questions and collects the application; it is branded as "your ING mortgage advisor".
Application data is stored in the bank's cloud region in the EU; the energy certificate is checked
by a third-party document-verification provider in the US.
