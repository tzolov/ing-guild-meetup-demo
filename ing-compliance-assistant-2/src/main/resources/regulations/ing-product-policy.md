# ING Product Approval Policy — FICTIONAL, written for the demo

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 2] Product Approval and Review Committee. No new product or material change to a product is offered to customers before the Product Approval and Review Committee has approved it. The submission includes the target market, the pricing, the customer journey, the risk assessment and the compliance checklist with every question answered and signed off by Compliance and Legal.

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 3] Model risk management. Every model that influences a customer outcome, such as pricing, acceptance, limits or fraud decisions, is registered in the model inventory, validated by Model Risk Management before first use, assigned an owner, and re-validated at least yearly or after any material change. Validation covers performance, stability, fairness across customer groups and explainability of individual outcomes.

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 4] Third-party and open-banking data. Transaction data obtained through account information services is used only for the purpose the customer consented to, for no longer than 90 days after the decision, and is never shared with a third party. Any third party that processes customer data is onboarded through Third-Party Risk Management, with a data-processing agreement and an approved data location.

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 5] Customer communication and AI assistants. Customer-facing AI assistants state at the start of the conversation that the customer is talking to an AI assistant, offer a route to a human colleague within one business day, never present themselves as a licensed advisor, and do not give personal financial advice. Automated decisions that are negative for the customer are explained in plain language and name the way to request a human review.

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 6] Sustainable products. A product that grants a benefit on the basis of a sustainability characteristic, such as an energy label, verifies that characteristic against an authoritative register (for Dutch homes, EP-Online) and not solely against a document supplied by the customer, and documents the verification for the audit trail.

[ING Product Approval Policy, Section 7] Logging and audit trail. Every automated decision about a customer is logged with the model version, the inputs used, the output and the human who reviewed it, if any. Logs are retained for seven years and are available to Internal Audit and to the regulator on request.
